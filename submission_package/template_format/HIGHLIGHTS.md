Highlights:

A stromal/cancer-associated-fibroblast program underlies gastric-cancer prognosis and is preserved across three external cohorts

A twenty-five-gene penalized Cox signature reaches leakage-free cross-validated concordance of 0.61, against 0.72 apparent

The signature adds negligible decision value beyond tumor stage, reported rather than overstated

Tumor-versus-normal microbiome differences are confounded with sequencing batch and do not replicate independently

Two-sample Mendelian randomization detects no causal effect of six microbial exposures on gastric-cancer risk
