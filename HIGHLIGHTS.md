# Highlights

- An externally-validated stromal/cancer-associated-fibroblast programme underlies gastric-cancer prognosis across three independent cohorts
- A 25-gene LASSO-Cox signature is prognostic but modest and honestly reported (nested cross-validation C = 0.61; not significant on meta-analysis)
- The prognostic co-expression module is strongly preserved externally (Zsummary 15.9–17.1) and localises to fibroblasts in single-cell data
- Apparent tumour-microbiome “dysbiosis” is a sequencing-batch artefact; Mendelian randomisation finds no causal microbial effect on gastric-cancer risk
